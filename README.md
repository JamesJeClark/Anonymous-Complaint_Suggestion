# Anonymous-Complaint_Suggestion
An anonymous complaint system or suggestion system for my school.
The purpose of this system is to allow students to send in Complaints & Solutions to higher ups ie "The hallways are too crowded. We should have "Random Class" leave a few minutes earlier so they can get to their classes easier.
Another purpose is to make the system have a suggestion tab that's sent to a system for the Student Council to be able to read without needing to constantly make google forms or similar to get the feedback when a student has the idea at that moment.
A main feature of this is to make the student's emails so that they don't need to worry about backlash but if the Suggestion or Complaint is too school inappropriate the higher ups and the student council can override the choice to see the email of the student that sent it in.
The reason the email is needed as there will be a system to keep track of how many complaints or suggestions that student has sent and then send that student an Email after their suggestion or complaint is looked at by the designated viewer.
